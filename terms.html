2. What the App does

Petits Savants is an educational app for the TV, made for children aged 2 to 9 (from the first year of French nursery school to CE2) and used under a parent's responsibility. Its lessons and exercises are based on the French school curriculum; it replaces neither school nor a teacher's advice. The parent chooses a parent PIN, creates the children's profiles and sets screen time, and remains responsible for how the children use the App.

3. Free access and Premium

The App is free. The first 2 lessons of every subject in every class, and some of the gifts for the animal, are available without a subscription. The Petits Savants Premium subscription ("Premium") opens every lesson and gift, for all the child profiles on the TV where it is active. Premium is bought in the Android TV app, through Google Play. On other TVs, the App is currently fully open without a subscription.

4. The Premium subscription

  • Purchase and payment: the subscription is bought from and paid to Google Play, with the TV's Google account, under Google Play's terms. The price, all taxes included, is shown in the App before purchase (in France: €4.99 a month or €39.99 a year).
  • Free trial: when offered, a 7-day free trial comes before the first paid period. It is for accounts that were never subscribed. Nothing is charged if the subscription is cancelled before the trial ends.
  • Renewal: the subscription renews automatically for the same length, at the current price, until it is cancelled.
  • Cancellation: at any time, in Google Play › Payments & subscriptions, or in the App (Espace parents › Gérer l'abonnement). It takes effect at the end of the current, already paid period, during which Premium stays available.
  • Refunds: they are requested from Google Play and handled under its rules, subject to your statutory rights.
  • Price changes: any price increase is announced in advance by Google Play; you can cancel before it applies.
  • New TV: Premium comes back on another TV signed into the same Google account, with « Restaurer mes achats ».
