Privacy policy

Petits Savants, a learning app for the TV · Updated: 20 October 2026

In short

  • No personal data is collected. Profiles, progress and settings stay on the TV.
  • No account, no ads, no usage analytics, no trackers, no third-party analytics services.
  • The phone connects to the TV over the home Wi-Fi, not through the internet.
  • The Premium subscription (Android TV) is paid through Google Play: we never see your payment details.

Who we are

What is stored, and where

To work, the app stores only in the TV's own storage:

  • the 4-digit parent PIN;
  • for each child: the first name typed by the parent, the class, the chosen animal and colour, the daily time limit and the subjects turned on;
  • progress: lessons finished, results, stars, badges, gifts, day streak, time spent each day;
  • settings (voice, sounds), the phone's connection code and the last known state of the Premium subscription.

This information is never sent to us or to anyone else: the app has no server and talks to no analytics service. It stays as long as the app is installed. You can erase it at any time: Espace parents › choose the child › « Recommencer à zéro » (start over) or « Supprimer le profil » (delete), or by uninstalling the app (or clearing its data in the TV's settings).

The phone

With the Android TV app, a phone can be used as a remote and a slate. It opens a page served by the TV itself on the local Wi-Fi network. What is tapped, typed (a first name, the parent PIN) or traced on the phone goes straight to the TV over that local network, not through the internet. The phone only remembers the TV's 4-digit code, to reconnect by itself. No app needs to be installed on the phone.

The voice

Instructions are read aloud by the TV's text-to-speech engine (Android's, LG's or Samsung's). The text to read is handed to it on the TV; how it is processed depends on that engine and its provider's policy. The voice can be turned off in Espace parents › Réglages.

The Premium subscription (Android TV)

The subscription is bought and managed by Google Play, with the TV's Google account. We receive neither your name, nor your email address, nor your payment details. On the TV, the app only asks Google Play whether a subscription is active, and acknowledges each purchase to Google Play as it requires. These exchanges are governed by Google's privacy policy. You manage or cancel the subscription in Google Play › Payments & subscriptions.

Children

Petits Savants is made for children aged 2 to 9 and follows Google Play's Families policy. The app collects no personal data from children, shows no ads, offers children no links to the outside and allows no contact with other people. The parent space and purchases are protected by the parent PIN.

Permissions (Android)

  • Internet / network: to serve the phone page on the home Wi-Fi, and for Google Play to handle the subscription.
  • Google Play billing: to offer the Premium subscription.

Your rights

As we hold no data about you, there is nothing to access, correct or delete on our side: the app's data is on your TV, under your control. You can write to [CONTACT EMAIL] with any question, and lodge a complaint with your data protection authority (in France, the CNIL: cnil.fr).

This website

This website uses no cookies, no analytics, and no fonts or scripts loaded from other sites. It is hosted by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.), which keeps technical logs, including visitors' IP addresses, to run the service and keep it secure, under GitHub's privacy statement.

Changes

Should the app ever process data differently, this page would be updated first, with a new date at the top.
